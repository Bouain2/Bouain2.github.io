Hello, my name is Omar. I first came to this world inside my grandfather's house kitchen, welcomed by two adoring parents: Mohand and Khdija. I am the youngest among three male siblings, Jamal and Hamid, without whom I could never dream of being the person I am today. 

I grew up in a small rural area called Laanoussar in the heart of the Middle Atlas. Between attending school and herding goats, I developed my curiosity to understand the wider world and dedicated my life to it. 

After wandering in the dimesnions of space and time, I find myself pursuing a PhD in Mathematics at McMaster University. My research explores the intersections of climate change, financial systems, and economic inequality in this happily miserable world.
